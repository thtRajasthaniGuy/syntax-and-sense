# 📚 Interview Preparation Roadmap

> A structured interview preparation roadmap for an experienced **React Native Developer** transitioning toward stronger frontend, full-stack, system design, and AI engineering skills.

> **Note**
>
> This roadmap assumes a solid understanding of JavaScript, TypeScript, React, and the React Native ecosystem. It does **not** cover beginner topics.

---

# 👋 About This Plan

I'm a **React Native Developer with 7+ years of experience**.

This repository documents my personal interview preparation journey.

My primary goals are:

- 📱 Keep React Native as my strongest skill.
- ⚛️ Target Frontend Engineer roles.
- 🏗️ Build strong Full-Stack engineering knowledge.
- 🤖 Learn AI and On-device LLM engineering.
- 💼 Prepare for senior-level software engineering interviews.

Rather than following a generic interview roadmap, this plan is tailored around:

- My existing experience
- Knowledge gaps
- Technologies I want to master
- Real-world projects that strengthen my resume
